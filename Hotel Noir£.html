Our Philosophy

A Hotel That
Feels Like Home

Hotel Noir£ was founded on a simple belief: luxury that lasts. Since opening in 2026, we have welcomed guests from over 80 countries into rooms designed for genuine rest — not just appearances.

Each of our 64 rooms are individually styled, our kitchen sources produce from farms within 60 kilometres, thus fresh, and our staff are trained to make every guest feel as though they never left home.
