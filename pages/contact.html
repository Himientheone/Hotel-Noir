Frequently Asked Questions

Check-in is from 2:00 PM and check-out is by 11:00 AM. Early check-in or late check-out can be arranged subject to availability — please contact us at least 24 hours in advance and we will do our best to accommodate you.

Classic and Deluxe rooms include complimentary breakfast for two guests at Noir£ Table. Suites include full breakfast and evening canapes. Additional guests can be added for KES 1,200 per person.

Cancellations made more than 48 hours before arrival receive a full refund. Cancellations within 48 hours are charged one night's room rate. No-shows are charged the full reservation amount.
