Noir£ Table

Farm-to-table cooking rooted in Kenyan ingredients and technique.

The Restaurant

Food That Knows
Where It Comes From

Noir£ Table seats 48 guests across a dining room and an open courtyard terrace. The menu changes every six weeks, but the philosophy stays constant: every ingredient must have Chef Grace Wanjiku's approval can name.

We partner with twelve farms within 60 kilometres of Nairobi. Our lamb comes from the Kajiado. Our coffee is single-origin from Kiambu County. Our bread is baked fresh every morning.
