The Restaurant

Food That Knows Where It Comes From

Noir£ Table seats 48 guests across a dining room and an open courtyard terrace. The menu changes every six weeks, but the philosophy stays constant: every ingredient must have Chef Grace Wanjiku's approval.

We partner with twelve farms within 60 kilometres of Nairobi. Our lamb comes from Kajiado. Our coffee is single-origin from Kiambu County. Our bread is baked fresh every morning.
