Who We Are

Our Story

Founded 2026

Built From a Single Conversation

Hotel Noir£ began in 2026 as an idea between two friends — Damien Mwotei, a Business man, and restaurateur Grace Wanjiku — seated at a Nairobi coffee shop, Fed up by a hospitality industry that had forgotten how to be genuinely hospitable.

Two years later, they opened the doors of a converted colonial-era building on Matundu Lane in Westlands. The building's 1940s bones — high ceilings, wide corridors, a central courtyard — were preserved and reimagined rather than demolished for something modern and impersonal.

Today Hotel Noir£ has 64 rooms, a flagship restaurant, a rooftop pool, and a small spa. But the founding question still guides every decision: "Is this the kind of place we would want to stay?"

What Guides Us

Our Three Pillars

Warmth

Every interaction — from check-in to check-out — is designed to feel personal. We train for empathy, not efficiency.

Rootedness

We are a Nairobi hotel, not a global brand that happens to be in Nairobi. Our art, our food, our staff — all deeply local.

Craft

Every material, every recipe, every piece of furniture was chosen with intention. Nothing here is accidental or inherited from a supplier catalogue.
